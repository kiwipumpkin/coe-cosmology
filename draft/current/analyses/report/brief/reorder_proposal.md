## Proposed (and applied) outline

1. **I Introduction** (setup and geometry): Figs 1-2
2. **II The fingerprint** and **III Method** (the inverse problem): Figs 3-4, Table I
3. **IV Fitting a few bodies: results and limits** (leftover vs N, examples, symmetry/walls, CoE core open, field solutions, patch ratio): Figs 5-13, Tables II-III
4. **V Comparison with observed structure** (how rare the field solutions would be): Figs 14-15, Table IV
5. **VI Growing and editing filled webs**: A sequential growth (new short subsection, Fig. 16); B editing rules, from ring backstop to entropy rule, and results: Figs 17-19, Table V
6. **VII Network rules: crystal analogy and the compensation rule**: Figs 20-21. It sits before the compensated web because the compensation rule is what the new generator is built on. That is why it isn't with the diagnostics, where the suggested outline put it.
7. **VIII A compensated random web reaches the CMB level** (headline): Figs 22-23, Table VI
8. **IX What is left: chaotic, noise-like texture** (moved up from the end): Figs 24-25
9. **X Structure diagnostics of the edited webs**: A mesh/area/energy (Figs 26-27); B pair energies and forces (Figs 28-29)
10. **XI Concentric shell models**: Figs 30-37
11. **Caveats, Conclusions, References**
12. **Appendices**: A sequential-placement universes (Figs 38-39, superseded); B further example universes 4-9 (Figs 40-41); C old->new figure-number table.
