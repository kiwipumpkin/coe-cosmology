## Notes

- signed masses as in the model: clusters +M (kg); voids = negative mass deficit (DELTA_V*rho*V, about -8.4e50 kg); so cluster-void links give U>0. U over mesh links only, 3D separations. gamma per mesh from its BEFORE cluster-cluster links (each cluster's mass shared over its cc links), held fixed. E_wall = gamma*v^2*perimeter (v=300 km/s) and gamma*c^2*perimeter (upper bound). k_B T_eff = mean 1/2 M v^2 of slab clusters. dF = dU + dE_wall - kT_eff dS_area. TOY ESTIMATE.
