## Morphology (what the plots show)

### Expanding universe — `|Vh|`
- Maxima near θ ≈ 0° and 180° (and 360°)
- Minima near θ ≈ 90° and 270°
- Vh x ~ cosine (amp ~100); Vh y ~ sine (amp ~70); avg |Vh| ~ 85–90

### Contracting universe — `|Vh|`
- Roughly inverted angular structure: maxima near θ ≈ 90–100° and 260–270°
- Minima near θ ≈ 0°/360° and 180°
- Vh y dominates amplitude; avg |Vh| ~ 75

This is the expand-vs-collapse shape fork: same class of off-center + gravity model, distinguishable sky curves. Migkas Fig. 6 longitude morphology is argued to match the **expanding** branch (max/min placement relative to the preferred axis).
