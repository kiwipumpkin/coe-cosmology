# Migkas Fig. 6 overlay

Digitised from Migkas et al. 2020 (arXiv:2004.03305) Fig. 6.

- CSV: `/workspace/cosmology/migkas/fig6_digitized.csv` (71 points, ~5° sampling of Δl=90° bins).
- Overlay: ST+Gauss tidal series $H_\mathrm{obs}/H=1+\alpha\kappa(3\mu^2-1)+\mathcal{O}(d/D)$, κ=0.15, α=1.0, d/D=0.1.
- Phase: φ=l so model quadrupole lows at l=90° and 270°; deep Migkas low near l~255–270°.
- Amplitude: model (H/H−1) scaled by 0.624 so peak-to-peak matches digitised A/Aall (illustrative comparable fractional amplitude).
- No dipole template.
- B/Ball panel: nearly flat, as in Migkas (slope stable).
