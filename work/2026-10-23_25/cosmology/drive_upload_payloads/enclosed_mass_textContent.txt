**M ≡ M_enc(<D)** = mass of mean-density matter in the ball of radius D (everything “between us and the center” in the spherical sense):

\[
M_\mathrm{enc}(<D) = \frac{4\pi}{3}\, \Omega_m\, \rho_c\, D^3 = \Omega_m\, M_\mathrm{crit}(<D).
\]

Dimensionless coupling used throughout prior work:

\[
\kappa \equiv \frac{G M}{D^3 H^2}.
\]

### 1.2 Analytic result

Critical density ρ_c = 3 H² / (8 π G) implies

\[
\frac{G M_\mathrm{crit}(<D)}{D^3 H^2} = \frac{1}{2},

\[
\boxed{\kappa_\mathrm{natural} = \frac{G M_\mathrm{enc}(<D)}{D^3 H^2} = \frac{\Omega_m}{2}}.

For **Ω_m = 0.3**:

\[
\kappa_\mathrm{natural} = 0.15.

## 2. Spherical enclosed-mass potentials — two interpretations

### 2.1 Uniform ball (literal M_enc with ρ = const)

Inside a uniform sphere (or infinite uniform medium with IR cutoff at ∼D), Newton:

\[
\mathbf{g}(r) = -\frac{4\pi G\rho}{3}\,\mathbf{r}, \qquad
\mathbf{u} = -\alpha\frac{\mathbf{g}}{H} = -\alpha\,\kappa\, H\, \mathbf{r}
\]

(with κ = (4π G ρ)/(3 H²) = Ω_m/2).

Observer at D, source at D + d n̂:

\[
[\mathbf{u}(r_s)-\mathbf{u}(D)]\cdot\hat{n} = -\alpha\,\kappa\, H\, d
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
H_\mathrm{obs} = H(1 - \alpha\kappa)
\]

**independent of n̂.** Numerical check (α=1, κ=0.15): monopole = −0.15; dipole A ∼ 10⁻¹⁷; half-ptp ∼ 0.

**Implication:** True spherical enclosed mass at mean density produces **zero sky anisotropy** — only a global H renormalization. It cannot be a Migkas dipole *or* quadrupole source by itself.

  \[

  \]
  pure **quadrupole**, C₂ → 2 α κ, C₁ → 0.
- Analytic series in ε (μ = n̂·D̂):

  \mathrm{frac} = \alpha\kappa(3\mu^2-1) + \alpha\kappa\,\varepsilon\,(4.5\,\mu - 7.5\,\mu^3) + O(\varepsilon^2).

  The O(ε) odd piece projects **purely onto P₃** (octupole): C₃ ≈ −3 α κ ε; **dipole Legendre C₁ = 0 at this order**.
