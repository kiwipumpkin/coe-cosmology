## Chris pushback (binding) — how this note answers it

1. **“Wouldn’t a quadrupole still have a dipole?”**  
   Yes, in the **observer-centric / Migkas sense**: a directional fit of \(A\cos\phi\) (or \(M+A\,\hat n\cdot\hat D\)) on a **mixed multipole field** or **partial sky** can return a sizable \(A\) even when the **full-sky Legendre dipole \(C_1\)** is consistent with zero.  
   That is **not** the same statement as “the orthogonal multipole expansion has a dipole.” Softened rule:  
   - Say **“full-sky Legendre \(C_1\approx 0\) at \(O(d/D)\) for the point-mass tidal field”** — true.  
   - Do **not** say **“a quadrupole has no dipole”** without distinguishing **multipole orthogonality** vs **fitted dipole on mixed/partial sky**.

2. **Unconstrained parameters.**  
   A free \(A\) in a pure-dipole template is under-constrained against quadrupole/octupole leakage and sky cuts; \(\chi^2\) / \(R^2\) must be reported, not only best-fit \(A\).

3. **Relative acceleration at different distances from CoE → Migkas angular curve.**  
   \(H_\mathrm{obs}=\Delta v_\mathrm{los}/d\) as a function of angle is exactly what we expand. Draft “Migkas-like” curves can appear from this kinematics; this note quantifies when that curve is **actually dipole-dominated** vs **quadrupole misread as dipole**.

**Setup (fixed):** point-mass proxy with \(M=M_\mathrm{enc}\), \(\kappa=\kappa_\mathrm{natural}=\Omega_m/2=0.15\) (\(\Omega_m=0.3\)), bolt-on \(\alpha=1\) primary; drag \(\alpha_\mathrm{eff}\approx 0.664\) scales amplitudes \(\propto\alpha\) (same morphology). Uniform-ball \(g\propto -r\) remains pure monopole (no anisotropy) — not re-scanned here.

## 1. Definitions (so “dipole” is not ambiguous)

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| \(\mu=\hat n\cdot\hat D\) | Cosine of angle from observer toward CoE |
| \(C_\ell\) | Legendre: \(\mathrm{frac}(\mu)=\sum C_\ell P_\ell(\mu)\), \(C_\ell=\frac{2\ell+1}{2}\int \mathrm{frac}\,P_\ell\,d\mu\) |
| \(A_\mathrm{moment}\) | \(\langle\mathrm{frac}\,\mu\rangle/\langle\mu^2\rangle\) (dipole moment; no monopole) |
| \(A_\mathrm{Migkas}\) | Coefficient of \(\mu\) in WLS \(\mathrm{frac}\approx M + A\mu\) |
| \(Q\) | Coefficient of \(P_2=(3\mu^2-1)/2\) in \(\mathrm{frac}\approx M + Q\,P_2\) |
| \(\chi^2\) proxy | \(ss_\mathrm{res}/ss_\mathrm{tot}=1-R^2\) (0 = perfect) |
| \(\phi\)-uniform | Equatorial ring, equal \(\Delta\phi\) — **not** Legendre measure |

**Orthogonality vs fit:** On the full sky with \(\mu\)-uniform (solid-angle) measure, \(P_1\perp P_2\). On a hemisphere or with \(\phi\)-uniform weights, that orthogonality is **broken** → quadrupole/octupole **leak into fitted \(A\)**.

## 3. Pure-dipole Migkas template \(A\cos\phi\) — when it works

Equatorial \(\phi\)-uniform fit \(\mathrm{frac}\approx M+A\cos\phi\), and \(\mu\)-uniform full-sky analogue. Near-CoE directions with \(r<0.05D\) masked at \(d/D\ge 1\).

| \(d/D\) | \(A\) (\(\phi\)-unif.) | \(R^2_\mathrm{dip}\) | \(\chi^2\) proxy | \(R^2_Q\) | Fourier \(a_1\) | Fourier \(a_2\) | Verdict |
|--------:|----------------------:|---------------------:|-----------------:|----------:|---------------:|---------------:|---------|
| 0.05 | \(-0.0085\) | 0.001 | 0.999 | **0.995** | \(\sim a_1\) small | \(\sim a_2\) large | **BAD** |

| 1.00 | \(0.271\) | 0.67 | 0.33 | 0.06 | dipole rising | | **MARGINAL** |
| 2.00 | \(0.098\) | **0.976** | 0.024 | 0.017 | dipole | | **GOOD** |

**φ-uniform octupole leakage (Chris mechanism, small-\(d/D\)):**  
Under equal-\(\Delta\phi\), \(P_3(\cos\phi)\) is **not** orthogonal to \(\cos\phi\):  
\(\langle P_3,\cos\phi\rangle/\langle\cos^2\phi\rangle = 3/8 = 0.375\).  
With \(C_3\approx -3\alpha\kappa\,(d/D)\),  
\[
A_\mathrm{leak}\approx 0.375\,C_3 \approx -0.017\quad(d/D=0.1),
\]  
matching the table. So a **nonzero fitted \(A\)** appears from **octupole**, while **Legendre \(C_1\) remains \(\approx 0\)**. Amplitude \(\sim 0.02\) is still **below** typical Migkas \(A\sim 0.05\)–\(0.15\) targets; it does **not** rescue a full-sky Migkas dipole, but it does forbid the slogan “quadrupole ⇒ fitted \(A=0\).”

**μ-uniform full sky:** \(A_\mathrm{Migkas}\approx 0\) and \(R^2_\mathrm{dip}\approx 0\) for all \(d/D\le 0.5\) (orthogonality restored).

### When is Migkas dipole a good description?

| Regime | Migkas \(A\cos\phi\)? |
|--------|----------------------|
| \(d/D\lesssim 0.2\) (beyond-horizon CoE + cluster depths) | **No** — use quadrupole (\(P_2\)) template |

## 6. Revised CONDITIONAL / smoking-gun wording

**Amplitude (enclosed mass):** natural — unchanged (\(\kappa_\mathrm{natural}=0.15\)).

**Morphology vs Migkas dipole — CONDITIONAL-NO (clarified):**

- **NO** as a **full-sky, beyond-horizon** prediction of spherical point-mass / uniform \(M_\mathrm{enc}\): leading anisotropy is **quadrupole** (or monopole for uniform ball); Migkas pure-dipole template has **\(R^2\approx 0\)** for \(d/D\lesssim 0.2\).
- **CONDITIONAL** if the analysis allows **sky selection**, **φ-uniform leakage**, or **\(d/D\gtrsim 1\)**: then a Migkas-sized **fitted** \(A\) appears without a true \(C_1\). That path is a **systematic / ontology** channel, not a clean CP smoking gun.
- **Open** for **non-spherical** mass (dipole mass moment, lopsided blast): not computed here.

**Program implication:** Do not claim “quadrupole ⇒ no Migkas dipole” bluntly. Do claim: **orthogonal \(C_1\) absent** at small \(d/D\); **Migkas dipole MCMC on full sky still NO-GO** for this spherical model; **footprint-induced fake \(A\sim 0.11\) (moment) / \(\sim 0.4\) (\(M+A\))** is quantified and must be folded into any Migkas comparison.
