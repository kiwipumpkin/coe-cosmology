Pure-dipole amplitude is suppressed by ∼10⁻⁶ at d/D=0.1 in the linear bolt-on map; forcing a dipole fit needs absurd κ∼10⁴. **Therefore:** amplitude alone is CONDITIONAL (borderline OK at few-Gpc D); **matching Migkas as a dipole with this Newtonian central-gravity mechanism is NO-GO**. ## 2. PRIMARY model — time-integrated gravity

### 2.1 Setup and approximations

1. Background ST: R_ST ∝ t^{2/5}, H=2/(5t), homologous r(t)=χ t^{2/5}.
2. Extra central point mass M with g=−GM/r² r̂ (not a self-consistent ST+gravity similarity solution; risk of double-counting blast mass).
3. Three treatments:
   - **(F) Frozen path, no drag:** Δv = ∫_{t_i}^{t_f} g(r_bg(t)) dt.
   - **(D) Hubble drag:** du/dt + H u = g on the homologous background.
   - **(O) Nonlinear ODE:** r̈ = −(6/25)r/t² − GM/r² (ST radial acceleration that preserves homology when M=0, plus gravity).
4. Observe at t_f: Euclidean H_obs = v_los/d, Ψ = H_obs − H.
5. Start time η = t_i/t_f = 10^{-3} (fiducial); integrals converge as η→0.

### 2.2 Analytic effective α vs bolt-on u = α GM/(r² H)

Along r ∝ t^{2/5}:

| Treatment | α_eff (η→0) | α_eff (η=10^{-3}) |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|
| Frozen ∫g dt | 2 | ≈ 1.50 |
| Drag du/dt+Hu=g | 2/3 | ≈ 0.66 |
| Bolt-on Phase A | α free ∼ O(1) | 1 (reference) |

**Consequence:** κ required for a fixed sky amplitude scales as 1/α_eff. Integration **cannot** reduce κ by orders of magnitude on this background.

### 2.3 Nonlinear ODE checks

- M=0: homology preserved to ∼10^{-5}; H_obs isotropic (null pass).
- Finite κ: ODE half-peak-to-peak amplitudes track the drag-effective bolt-on within ∼ tens of percent (same κ ballpark).
