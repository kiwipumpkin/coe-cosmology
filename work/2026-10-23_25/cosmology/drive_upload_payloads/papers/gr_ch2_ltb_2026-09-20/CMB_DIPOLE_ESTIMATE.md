# Geometric CMB dipole estimate (off-center CoE)

## Formula

Along the axis, path lengths from observer at distance D to a CoE-centered shell of radius R_CMB are (R+D) toward the CoE and (R-D) away. The fractional path asymmetry is D/R_CMB.

First estimate:

    (δT/T)_geom ≈ α (D / R_CMB) (ΔH/H)_eff

with α ~ 0.5–2.

If contrast is localized within radius L < D of the CoE (anti-CoE ray misses the core):

    (δT/T)_geom ≈ (ΔH/H) (L / R_CMB)

## Observed anchor

v ≈ 370 km/s ⇒ δT/T ≈ 1.23e-3.

## Migkas-scale ΔH/H

~0.06 (Amp×sin on sheet A) to ~0.09 (Migkas 2021 ~9% H0).

## Allowed window (α=1, ΔH/H=0.06)

D/R_CMB ≲ 0.021 ⇒ R_CMB/D ≳ 49.

If R_CMB ~ 14 Gpc, D ≲ ~0.29 Gpc (~290 Mpc). Larger than classic Alnes ~15 Mpc because contrast is only ~6–9%, not order unity. Still incompatible with D/R ~ O(0.1–0.5) without cancellation.
