# Expanding vs collapsing angular curves

**Setup:** Same two-scale density (ρ(D)=0.3 ρ_crit, κ≈0.23). Homologous background H_bg = +|H| (expand) or −|H| (collapse). Gauss peculiar uses timescale |H| (gravity still toward CoE).

**Why shapes differ:** Pure tidal δv is the same; signed H_obs = H_bg + δv/d sits near +1 vs −1. Observable A ∝ 1/H_obs² is a nonlinear map, so expand and collapse produce different angular A(φ) after mean renormalization.

**vs Migkas A (phase + scale only):** expand R²≈-0.561, collapse R²≈-0.349.

**Chris’s point:** both can look “accelerated” in a bulk sense from relative v and a; the sky curve morphology selects expansion (Migkas).
