6    Time evolution and the CMB
In the donut-universe picture the CoE is fixed while thick shells expand outward faster than light in a coordinate
sense; the sphere of events visible to us grows at c. Recombination is a spherical surface of fixed physical radius
around the CoE. The observer remains mid-shell between the inner and outer edges (S1 and S2). The Hubble-
bubble volume about US satisfies VHB = 0 until the observer passes Drecomb ; afterward the visible sphere grows
at c. Scale may be tracked through the volumes VHB (t), VS1 (t), and VS2 (t) and their fractions. Frames A–D
show shells crossing that recombination surface while the visible sphere grows more slowly.
