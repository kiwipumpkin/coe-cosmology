2.2   Fitting procedure
The analysis proceeds in five steps.

  1. Compare Migkas A/Aall versus Galactic longitude ℓ (their Fig. 6; our Fig. 2), and fit by redshift slice (our
     Fig. 3), with orientation against Migkas Fig. 8.
  2. Fit a CoE plus thick shell in both Newtonian gravity and relativistic LTB[4, 5, 6].
  3. Orient the model on the Galactic sky: untilted, then +15◦ tilt, then a radial gradient, then an +80.8◦
     roll about the CoE axis, matching Migkas Fig. 8.
  4. Evolve the donut-universe picture—shells expanding outward faster than light in a coordinate sense, while
     the sphere of events visible to us grows at c, with recombination as a spherical surface of fixed physical
     radius around the CoE.
  5. Stress-test the CMB: compute the gravitational multipole on the last-scattering sky, form the kill function
     (the electromagnetic temperature multipole opposite in sign to that gravitational multipole), and compare
     exploratory two-emitter and multipole-partner constructions.
