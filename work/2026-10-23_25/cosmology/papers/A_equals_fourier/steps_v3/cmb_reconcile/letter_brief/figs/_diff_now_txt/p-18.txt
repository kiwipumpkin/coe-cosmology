Shell expansion and pointing at the CoE
The LTB shell equation integrates to the usual early radiation and late matter asymptotics, with the sign of
E(r) fixing whether a dust shell expands forever or recollapses. Figure A1 collects those solutions for a flat
matter+radiation track, dust with E = 0, E > 0, and E < 0.
