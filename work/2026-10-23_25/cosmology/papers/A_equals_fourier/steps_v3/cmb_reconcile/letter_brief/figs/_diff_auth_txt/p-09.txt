The same off-centre geometry produces a gravitational multipole on the last-scattering sky. The kill function
is the electromagnetic temperature multipole—∆T /T of kinematic–Doppler character—required to cancel that
multipole: opposite in sign, same angular shape. Exploratory two-emitter and multipole-partner constructions
follow the kill-function morphology at large D and remove most of the demeaned power through ℓ ≤ 4, but
they do not yet reach the CMB bound of order 10−5 . Closing that residual on the full light cone remains the
open calculation.
