The untilted-to-tilted-to-rolled sequence
places the CoE axis on the Galactic sky.
With a +15◦ tilt alone, the model
MAX/MIN bands already track the
large-scale extrema of Migkas Fig. 8.
Adding a radial gradient sharpens the
contrast between near-axis and mid-latitude
bands without changing the CoE tilt. The
final +80.8◦ roll about the CoE axis then
rotates the MAX/MIN pattern into
agreement with the Migkas Fig. 8
orientation.
Fit parameters for D, Ωs , and ℓ0 are held
fixed through this sequence; only the sky
placement of the already-fitted geometry is
varied. The colour scale is the Migkas
A/Aall field; coloured bands are the
predicted MAX/MIN loci at the listed θ
centres, with point markers for the CoE,
anti-CoE, and measured extrema.
