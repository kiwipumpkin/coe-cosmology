In the concentric-shell geometry an observer between Rin and Rout sees an angular disk toward the CoE in
which lines of sight hit the inner shell only, eclipsing the outer shell. For the illustrative ratios Rin = 1,
Rout = 3, D = 1.8, the half-angle is α = arcsin(Rin /D) ≈ 33.7◦ . Outside that disk, rays strike the outer shell.
This eclipse partition selects which emitting surface contributes along each direction and therefore enters the
Doppler multipole that must cancel the gravitational kill.
