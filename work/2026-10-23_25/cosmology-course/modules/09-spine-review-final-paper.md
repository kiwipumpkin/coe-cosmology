## 5. Worked example final paper / memo

*Model answer — not unique. Deliberately kills over-ambitious geometry, then states a narrower claim. Neither ΛCDM nor CoE advocacy.*

### A. Claim

Horizon-scale CoE offsets with \(D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}}\sim 0.3\) at Migkas-sized contrast (\(\sim 6\%\)) are **ruled out** by the CMB geometric dipole budget; a viable CoE-style sketch, if any, must keep \(D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}}\lesssim 0.02\) and still face an independent cluster-dipole test plus kSZ profile limits.

### B. Geometry priors

Spherical, centrally concentrated decreasing \(\rho(r)\); homologous outward expansion; observer at \(D\); vision shell \(r\sim\) few\(\times 10^2\,\mathrm{Mpc}\); CMB as CoE-centered sphere \(R_{\mathrm{CMB}}\gg D\). Gauss/shell: \(M(<r)\) acts; exterior shells cancel. Uniform-ball \(\rho\) rejected. **Not claimed:** Bertschinger (1983) shocks *are* this universe; Newtonian \(R^2\) to digitised Migkas is GR; “no center” rhetoric is false because a center would feel fairer.

### C. Primary test

| Field | Entry |
|-------|-------|
| Observable | Sky dipole amplitude of inferred local expansion from X-ray cluster \(L_X\)–\(T\) normalisation (jointly \(\Delta H/H\) or bulk-flow equivalent) |
| Dataset | Migkas et al. (2020, 2021) as *current* measurement; **pass/fail for the sketch** deferred to independent all-sky X-ray clusters (eROSITA-era) repeating \(L_X\)–\(T\) directional analysis |
| Null (CP/FLRW) | Dipole \(\approx 0\) after cleaning, or residual = bulk flow / calibration with velocity-like redshift dependence |
| CoE-style prediction | Stable dipole with \((\Delta H/H)_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim 0.06\)–\(0.09\), axis tied to CoE, **only if** \(D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}}\lesssim 0.02\) |
| Pass | Independent sample recovers same-hemisphere dipole with \(0.04\leq(\Delta H/H)_{\mathrm{eff}}\leq 0.12\) at \(\geq 3\sigma\) after pre-registered cuts, **and** CMB gate satisfied |
| Fail / kill | (i) Independent amplitude \(<0.03\) (95% CL), or (ii) vanishes under pre-registered X-ray stress test, or (iii) required \(D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}}>0.02\), or (iv) axis jumps \(\gtrsim 60^\circ\) between independent samples |
| Systematics | Galactic absorption; \(T\) calibration; Malmquist/flux anisotropy; bulk-flow vs \(H\) degeneracy; CMB apex mismatch |

### D. Dipole budget gate

\((\Delta H/H)_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.06\), \(\alpha=1\), demand \((\delta T/T)_{\mathrm{geom}}\leq 1.2\times 10^{-3}\):

\[
D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}} \lesssim 1.2\times 10^{-3}/0.06 = 0.02.
\]

If \(R_{\mathrm{CMB}}\sim 14\,\mathrm{Gpc}\), \(D\lesssim 0.28\,\mathrm{Gpc}\).  
**Kill now:** \(D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}}=0.3\) ⇒ \((\delta T/T)_{\mathrm{geom}}\sim 0.018\) ≈ **\(15\times\)** observed dipole scale — fail before any Migkas re-analysis.

### E. Alnes–Kodama and kSZ

**Alnes–Kodama.** Alnes & Amarzguioui (2006): \(\sim 15\,\mathrm{Mpc}\) for **order-unity Gpc** LTB voids. Our contrast is Migkas-sized, so the number does not copy-paste; the lesson does: dipole scales as \((D/R)\times\)contrast. Kodama et al. (2010): bounds stay \(\ll\) Gpc. This memo refuses \(D\sim\) Gpc at \(10\%\)-class contrast without a full-sky cancellation calculation (not assumed).

**kSZ.** García-Bellido & Haugbølle (2008): deep voids produce large CMB dipoles in off-center cluster frames → kSZ. Sketch either keeps contrast shallow on Gpc scales or accepts kSZ non-detection as fail. Until a cited kSZ bound is mapped to the density profile, the sketch is **not cleared**, only **not immediately dead** on Alnes arithmetic alone.

### F. Spine synthesis

Depends on Module 1 (rhetoric vs CP; decreasing \(\rho\)), Module 4 (bulk-flow degeneracy), Module 5 (Migkas caveats; \(R^2\) not proof), Module 6 (dipole gate), Module 7 (Alnes–Kodama + kSZ), Module 8 (explicit inhomogeneity rather than Buchert slogan).

### G. Honesty clause

Abandon CoE if independent clusters drive \((\Delta H/H)_{\mathrm{eff}}<0.03\) under pre-registered cuts, or if needed amplitude forces \(D/R_{\mathrm{CMB}}>0.02\) without calculated cancellation that survives CMB maps. Say CP/FLRW remains adequate if the independent cluster dipole is consistent with zero (or pure bulk flow) and no leftover geometric CMB term is required by late-time data.

### H. Secondary check

Secrest et al. (2021, 2022) number-count dipole vs kinematic CMB expectation: optional axis correlation with cluster \(H\) dipole; not the same observable; systematics live.
