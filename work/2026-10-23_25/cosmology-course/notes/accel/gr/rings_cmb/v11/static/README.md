# Static-space (no expansion) scenario, Oct 19 2026
Interpretation: fixed Euclidean space; matter radial ballistic from CoE at t=0 (v ∝ r with no mass, no speed cap; Newtonian point core mass otherwise);
light straight at c in the fixed frame; emission at own crossing of fixed R_rec = 0.5 D (plasma inside opaque); classical two-body Doppler.
Scripts: static.py (model), feasibility.py (-> feasibility.json), migkas_galilean_check.py, run_best.py (-> best.pkl), plot_rays.py, plot_dtt.py.
Figures: static_rays_2x2.png, static_dTT_360.png, static_redshift_vs_angle.png.
Key results: only look directions within 30° of the CoE (6.70% of sky) can ever receive recombination light; today's sky is empty for speed ≥ 2c (no mass) / 1.944c (mass 0.0154);
above 1c all received light was overtaken by us; with no mass classical Doppler reduces exactly to 1+z = t_today/t_emission; with mass 1+z passes through 0 and ∞ (159.2°–161.9° at 1.052c).
