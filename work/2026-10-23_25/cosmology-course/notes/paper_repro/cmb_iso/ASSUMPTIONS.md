# cmb_iso: isotropic emission at R_rec crossing (assumptions)
Sources: /tmp/a.txt (Chris's paper text): D = 58.2 Gpc, annulus D−d..D+d = 43.9..72.5 Gpc (lines 285–287; CatWISE-locked fit value, used as geometry); vision radius R = 14.3 Gpc (line 164, "fixed ... not fitted"); Milne age t0 = 13.97 Gyr (line 1121). R_rec is NOT given numerically in the paper; 24.5 Mpc is taken from earlier chat work (stretch-to-2.725 K estimate), not from the paper.
A1 Kinematics: everything coasts from the CoE at t=0, v = r/t. Matter with speed v crosses R_rec at t_c = R_rec/v and is now at r_now = vT.
A2 Emission: each matter element releases 3000 K photons isotropically once, at its R_rec crossing event (position R_rec·n̂, time t_c). The R_rec sphere does not shine as a wall.
A3 Expansion share f: a(t) ∝ t^f, so H = f/t. Motion through space = (1−f)·v. Light moves at c relative to local space, so its path is a straight line in comoving coordinates, comoving distance ∫c dt/a. The crossing point's comoving radius is R_rec·(T/t_c)^f.
A4 Ray trace: for each sky direction, find every t_c where the backward ray from the observer (at D now) meets the comoving image of the R_rec sphere. Each root is an emission event, with emit time t_c, receive time T, and travel delay T − t_c recorded.
A5 Weight of each event: (density at crossing ρ_now·λ³) × (crossing speed v) / |d(miss distance)/dt_c|, a thin-emitting-surface heuristic, with λ = r_now/R_rec. The density law ρ is defined on matter's present radius. The schematic 3..9 laws are mapped linearly onto 43.9..72.5 Gpc and are zero outside it. The step law is 1 on the annulus and 5 inside 43.9 Gpc. DIAG_uniform_everywhere (ρ=1 at all radii) is a diagnostic only, not a Chris law.
A6 Pieces per photon: stretch = a(T)/a(t_c) = (T/t_c)^f (cube root of the volume growth of its region under homologous a). Doppler = classical (1 − u_o·k̂)/(1 − u_s·k̂), with u = (1−f)·velocity relative to space. An event is rejected (undefined) if either factor is ≤ 0. SR is not used because speeds are far above c. Gravity = Newtonian Φ of ρ with total mass M (Gpc units, G/c² = 4.785e-20 Gpc/M☉), with Φ at emission scaled homologously (λΦ_now(r_now)) and weak-field ν_o/ν_e = 1 + (Φ_e − Φ_o)/c². Flagged as failing when |Φ|/c² > 0.1. Events with factor ≤ 0 are rejected.
A7 Direction temperature: bolometric blend T_dir = (Σ w T_i⁴ / Σ w)^{1/4} (intensity-weighted, blackbody). No overall brightness normalisation.
A8 Stats (sinθ-weighted mean, cosθ dipole, rms residual) only when every direction is defined. Otherwise "partial sky, stats not computed". Plots keep the dipole.
A9 Masses 0, 1e12, 1e15, 1e18, 1e20, 1e22 M☉. Mass does not decelerate the kinematics (inconsistent at high M).
